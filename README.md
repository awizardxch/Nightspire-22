# Nightspire-22

The concept home for the Nightspire market tower on **lot-22, Panda Park** —
the flagship build of the Nightspire Exchange, the Musebook-to-Musebucks
peer-to-peer marketplace.

## The site

- **Panda Park ward** — cultural, ring 1, the tallest district in town
  (heightCap 3), rate 40 Musebucks/sq
- **lot-22**: 18 squares, x 8..10 / z 28..33 — one row of parkland back from
  the lake (water edge at z26, lake to the south)
- The town is a **flat grid** — no mountains or hilltops. Elevation here is
  vertical only, which is exactly why a tower reads.
- Floors allowed: **4** (3 by right + 1 via works raise)

## The tower

1. **Floor 1 — Forge + marketplace.** Metal-bodied, obsidian-enchanted tools
   (glassy obsidian panes in forged metal, glowing seams — never pure
   obsidian), market stalls, order boards pinned with offer slips, and the
   **Spellbook** floating over the central counter: the glowing ledger whose
   cyan runes record the two-leg MUSEBOOK/Musebucks settlement. The book that
   keeps the market honest.
2. **Floor 2 — Balcony.** Smaller footprint, lake views facing south over the
   water.
3. **Floor 3 — Living quarters.** Warm-lit windows.
4. **Floor 4 — The Nightspire.** Dark silhouette rimmed in cyan, orange glow
   at its tip like a forge-heart carried skyward.

## Visual language

Nightspire theme tokens: deep-space backgrounds `#060810` / `#0a0c18`, cyan
accent `#00d9ff`, inner orange glow `#ff6600`, far purple `#1a082b`. The
**full moon** 🌕 — the brand mark — hangs over the lake.
